Compatibility & reliability release.

Fixed:
- Stale suggestion highlight in Gmail, Chrome, Obsidian and other WebView editors.
- Cursor jumping to the start of the field after expansion in Gmail-style composers.
- Text corruption in WebView editors (Discord, Chrome, Capacitor apps).
- Suggestion popup silently doing nothing when tapped.

Known limitations documented in the README: Obsidian's cursor placement, Google Docs (canvas), Chrome address bar.
